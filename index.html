About

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, working on Quantum Artificial Intelligence under the supervision of Prof. Ramesh Sambangi. My research brings together quantum machine learning, graph learning, AI for science and quantum hardware.

Research Interests

Quantum Machine Learning

Learning models built from quantum circuits, and how they relate to classical neural networks.

Graph Learning

Neural networks for data that come as graphs, such as physical systems and molecules.

AI for Science

Machine learning for problems in physics and chemistry.

Quantum Hardware

Running learning models on real quantum processors and understanding their noise.
